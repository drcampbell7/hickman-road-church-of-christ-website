First Visit

What to Expect

Walking into a new church for the first time can feel daunting. You are genuinely welcome here, no matter where you are coming from.

Our services run 60–70 minutes and include congregational a cappella singing, prayer, a Bible-based sermon, and the Lord's Supper. Dress is casual to business casual — come as you are.

A friendly greeter will meet you at the door. Children's Bible classes are available at 9:30 AM for ages 0–18 and an adult Bible class is available as well.

Core Convictions

Our Beliefs

Statement of Faith

This statement summarizes our core beliefs. The Bible—God’s inspired and infallible Word—is our final authority for truth, morality, and conduct.

Bible

We believe the Bible is God’s inspired Word. The sixty‑six books of Scripture are His complete and authoritative revelation. Traditions and writings may offer insight, but none equal Scripture.

God

We believe in one eternal God who exists in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. God is love and created us for relationship. He has revealed Himself in many ways, most directly through the incarnation—God the Son taking on flesh.

Jesus

We believe Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God. He became flesh, fulfilled God’s redemptive plan, died on the cross, and rose bodily on the third day. He ascended to heaven as our High Priest and will return for final judgment and redemption.

Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit co‑exists with the Father and the Son. He indwells believers at baptism, guaranteeing Christ’s return. He guides, helps, and sanctifies God’s people.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in the gospel of Jesus’ death, burial, and resurrection. This message calls people to believe, repent, confess Jesus as Lord, and be baptized—reenacting Christ’s saving work, as first preached in Acts 2.

Church

We believe the church is God’s family made up of all who are saved. Jesus promised to build His church, and all who are born again become part of this “called‑out” people.

Eternity

We believe Jesus will return. Until then, we wait with hope. At His coming, God’s people—living and dead—will be gloriously transformed.
